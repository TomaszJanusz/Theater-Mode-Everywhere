# AMO Listing - en_GB

## Name
Theater Mode Everywhere

## Summary
Press T to enter theatre mode and fill the window. Subtitles and Volume Boost, plus chat and skip buttons on popular services.

## Description
Press T to enter theatre mode: the video fills your browser window, with no headers, sidebars, comments or recommendations to distract you.

Theater Mode Everywhere works with the video players on most websites, including videos embedded in other pages.

TL;DR
- Simple controls on most sites: subtitles, speed and optional Volume Boost up to 300%.
- Remove the black bars with Fill or Stretch, or move the video up to make room for subtitles.
- Rich Theater Experience adds extras on YouTube, Twitch, Netflix and Disney+: chat beside the video, skip buttons, chapters and previews.
- Free, open source, no analytics: nothing about you or what you watch is sent to the developer.

EXTRA FEATURES ON POPULAR SERVICES
Rich Theater Experience (experimental) brings features of each service into theatre mode. Turn it on or off for each service in the toolbar popup or Settings.
- YouTube: chat beside the video (live or replay), chapters, timeline previews, Most Replayed moments, audio tracks and recommended videos when the video ends.
- Twitch: chat beside the video (live or replay), plus previews and subtitles on past broadcasts.
- Netflix: Skip Intro and Next Episode buttons, plus dubbed, original and audio description tracks.
- Disney+: Skip and Next Episode buttons, plus audio tracks including audio description.
Also supported:
- Vimeo: chapters, previews and the next video in a showcase.
- Patreon: subtitles, chapters and previews.
- SOOP (sooplive.com): chat beside the video and the streamer's name in the title; on recordings, chapters and the previous and next recording.
- Crunchyroll: intro and credits markers, previews, Skip and Next Episode buttons, and subtitle choice (subtitles built into the video stay on).
- Bilibili: subtitles (some need a Bilibili login), chapters, previews, playlists and recommended videos when the video ends.
- Bilibili.tv: subtitles, previews, intro and outro markers, and Skip and Next Episode buttons on episodes.
- Tencent Video / WeTV: subtitles, previews and the next episode.
- iQIYI (iq.com): series and episode titles, subtitles and audio tracks, intro and outro markers, and the previous and next episode.
- Rakuten Viki: every subtitle language of the episode, audio tracks on dubbed episodes, previews, and Skip Intro and Next Episode buttons.

KEY FEATURES
- Video fit: Z switches between Fit, Fill (crops the edges) and Stretch. On screens taller than the video, Raised (Shift+L) moves it up, with subtitles in the space below.
- Subtitles and audio: pick subtitle and audio tracks, turn subtitles on or off with C, and style the font, size, colour and background. Your languages are remembered.
- Optional sound effects: Volume Boost up to 300%, Voice boost, Reduce loud sounds and Bass.
- Keyboard (in theatre mode): YouTube-style speed keys, hold Right arrow for 2x speed, comma and full stop to step frame by frame, and W, A, S and D to move between controls. H lists every shortcut; all can be changed.
- Controls and look: the video title, volume and speed sliders, and a Replay screen when the video ends. Shift+H keeps the controls on screen. Choose an accent colour, High contrast, Reduce transparency or Reduce motion. 15 interface languages.
Note: features vary by service and video. Sound effects, including Volume Boost, do not work on DRM-protected video, such as Netflix and Disney+, or where a site blocks them.

HOW TO START
After installing, open a page with a video and press T (not while typing in a text field). Shift+T switches between several videos; T or Escape leaves theatre mode. You can also add an optional button on videos (beta), or turn on AutoTheater to enter theatre mode automatically on services with Rich Theater Experience enabled, after a countdown you can cancel. Turn the extension off for any site in the toolbar popup. An optional short setup asks how you like to watch.

PRIVACY, FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Nothing about you or what you watch goes to the developer or any unrelated third party. There are no analytics. The extension needs access to all websites to find videos, but keeps no list of the pages you visit. Some features load subtitles and previews directly from the service you watch. Settings, including excluded sites and per-site languages, can sync through your browser account. The privacy policy is in Settings, under About.
Theater Mode Everywhere is free and always will be. It is open source under the MIT licence.
