Theater Everywhere — Test Page

Use this page to test the key bindings and theater mode styling inside your browser viewport.

How to test:

  1. Start playing the video below.
  2. Press the T key on your keyboard to expand the video player to fill the browser window.
  3. Use CC to switch subtitle tracks, and hover the timeline for chapter titles.
  4. Press the T key again or the Escape key to restore the standard layout.
  5. Try toggling the extension on/off for this page via the toolbar popup and manage settings.
