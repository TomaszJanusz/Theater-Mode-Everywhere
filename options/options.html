Features

Turn optional theater mode behavior on or off.

Enter theater mode when the main video starts playing on supported services’ watch pages with Rich Theater Experience enabled.

Keep the playback controls and progress bar visible in theater mode. The title and cursor still hide when idle.

Amplify volume up to 300% using Web Audio API. A brief loading spinner may appear the first time you enter theater mode while this is enabled.

Show markers from the installed SponsorBlock extension on the YouTube theater timeline, using its colors.

Hi,

Thank you so much for choosing my extension! Theater Mode Everywhere was born out of my frustration with the many different solutions that were all supposed to provide one thing: a comfortable way to watch video. They don't deliver it. Most players on the internet lack features that should be standard.

That's why I created Theater Mode Everywhere: a universal player that, in my humble opinion, gives you a comfortable way to watch video everywhere, and can handle the non-standard, unique features of many websites.

The extension is free and doesn't send any information about you or what you watch anywhere. If something doesn't work right, you can report it on GitHub.

Theater Mode Everywhere is available on the Chrome Web Store, Mozilla Add-ons and Microsoft Edge Add-ons. I'd be happy if you shared your opinion about it.

Happy watching!
